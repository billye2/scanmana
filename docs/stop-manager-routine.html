Stop manager routine

Scanmana · daily bars · decide at night, act at the open

The entry does not guarantee success. Take every loss at the stop. Let the winners run to the exit.

Every night, after the scan

  1. 1
    Read the tape first. Bullish or Not bullish. On a weak tape the app cuts size to half or a quarter. Home, market bar · /tape
  2. 2
    Go through the deck. A boxed Wait card resting under its box top is the shape. The pick alone will not carry the trade. Home deck, plan box on each card
  3. 3
    Know the stop before you buy. The stop is the breakout day's low. Wider than one ADR: skip the card. Stop ladder beside Take
  4. 4
    Size from the stop, never the other way round. Then tap Take or Take at open. Deck card · /sim prices a trade opened without a plan

The entry

  1. 5
    Buy the open after the first close above the box. No close above it in ten sessions means no trade. "At the open" line · nightly push
  2. 6
    The stop is set the moment you are filled. It never moves down.

While the position is open

  1. 7
    Read the Tomorrow box every night. It gives the action, the stop, and the close that must hold. /paper, under each open position
  2. 8
    A trade at or under the stop sells everything. That is a normal trade at −1R. Do not widen the stop to avoid it.
  3. 9
    Day 4, if the prior close is above the entry: sell half at the open. Then raise the stop to the entry. The trade can no longer lose. /paper · Sell at next open, then Stop · Raise
  4. 10
    Hold the rest until a close under the 10-day average. Sell at the next open. This is where the profit comes from. /paper · Sell at next open

Each week

  1. 11
    Drill. Take or Skip on a covered chart, place the stop, see four stops on one trade. /drill
  2. 12
    Check the routine, not the last trade. Where the R leaks, the exit grid, and the fan. /studies · /paper fan card

Size

shares = 1% of cash ÷ (entry − stop)

Never

  • Move a stop down.
  • Buy when the stop is wider than one ADR.
  • Sell a winner before the day-4 half or the 10-day close.
  • Decide during the session. Decide at night.
  • Judge the routine on fewer than 30 trades.

Remember

  • Three to five losses in a row is normal at 40%.
  • A wide stop survives more and pays fewer R on the same move.
  • One R is what you risked. Count in R, not dollars.
  • The Auto ledger runs steps 9 and 10 by itself. On the Manual ledger they are yours.
